A fair question

Why not just download a trading skill for an AI?

You can find free add-ons that let an AI model place trades for you. Here is what we do that an add-on on its own doesn’t.

  • We test before we trade.

    Each idea’s pass marks are written down before it runs. It is then checked on market data nobody has looked at yet.

  • We count the costs.

    Every figure we work from is after trading costs. Costs are what sank most of our own ideas.

  • We publish what failed.

    Fourteen ideas so far, ten of them retired, each with its reason on the What we tested page.

  • We proved the off switch works.

    Practice money first, fixed limits on every trade, and a stop button we have pressed during market hours to check it closes everything.

A downloaded skill can give an AI a way to place trades. Whether those trades were ever worth placing is a separate question, and it is the one we’re working on.
